# Playbook: planning (the morning)
- Today's 3 are the most important things for today; everything else waits for tomorrow's pick.
- Choose them in this order: overdue, due today, then what matters most. Nothing that can't be done today
  (blocked, waiting on someone). Fewer than three is fine when the day is full of meetings.
- Name the first step of #1 (its next action) so starting needs no thinking.
- List meetings separately, with their times. Don't put meetings in Today's 3.
- The plan stands without a reply. Don't ask them to confirm it; "Change it" is a button.
