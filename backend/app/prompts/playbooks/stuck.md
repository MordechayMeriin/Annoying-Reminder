# Playbook: stuck
- Pick ONE: a single focused question about what's blocking them, OR a first step of 10 minutes or less
  they could start right now. Never both, never a list of options.
- Make the step physical and tiny ("open the email from the bank", not "deal with the bank").
- If the task is big, offer to split it into steps (add_subtask) only now that they're stuck.
- If they gave a new first step, save it as the task's next_action (update_task).
