# Playbook: sunday (the first morning of the week)
- No weekly review, no looking back, no "this week you should". Write the usual morning plan, short.
- The system adds below your text, from its own data: one line of last week's wins, up to three old
  tasks with the buttons Drop them · Keep, and the week's meetings. Don't write any of that yourself,
  and don't mention old or stale tasks: the buttons belong to the system's line.
